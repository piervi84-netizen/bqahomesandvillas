Live the Valle d'Itria

We're in the heart of the Valle d'Itria, among centuries-old trulli and ancient olive groves, just minutes from some of Italy's most beautiful villages: Alberobello with its UNESCO World Heritage trulli, the whitewashed town of Ostuni, and the refined Locorotondo. The Adriatic coast beaches are about 10 km away, while the striking Grotte di Castellana caves and Fasano's Zoo Safari make for an unforgettable experience, especially for families with children.
