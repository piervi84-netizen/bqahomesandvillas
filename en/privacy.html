Privacy and cookies

Last updated: 27 November 2026.

Who we are

The website bqahomesandvillas.com is run by BQA Homes and Villas. For any request about your data, write to bqaboutiquehomesandvillas@gmail.com.

Analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4, a Google service, to understand how the site is used (pages visited, where visitors come from, clicks on WhatsApp, email, phone and on links to Booking.com and Airbnb). These cookies are enabled only if you press “Accept” in the banner. If you press “Decline” or make no choice, Google Analytics is not loaded. IP addresses are anonymised. Sharing of data with Google for other purposes is switched off.

You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser: the banner will appear again.

Technical storage

The site stores on your device only your cookie choice, to remember it on your next visit. They are not used to profile you.

Enquiries and bookings

If you write to us on WhatsApp or by email, we use the details you send (name, dates, request) only to reply and manage your booking. WhatsApp and email providers process data under their own policies.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction or deletion of your data and object to processing by writing to the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy: the Garante, garanteprivacy.it).
