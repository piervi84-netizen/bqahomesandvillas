BQA Fracassini Rooms Rome-Flaminio

Looking for a room near the Stadio Olimpico, in the Flaminio neighborhood? Rooms in Flaminio, Rome: three room types with private bathroom and kitchen, steps from Piazza del Popolo and the Stadio Olimpico.

The rooms

  • Standard Queen — ideal for couples or solo travelers
  • King Standard — more space, king-size bed
  • Queen Quad — two queen beds, for families or groups
  • Air conditioning, Smart TV and free WiFi in every room
  • Private en-suite bathroom, kettle and coffee machine
  • Fully equipped kitchen (fridge, oven, microwave, stovetop) and washing machine
  • Free crib available on request

The area

  • A 10-minute walk from Piazza del Popolo
  • Stadio Olimpico and Auditorium Parco della Musica within walking distance
  • Flaminio metro station (Line A) a few minutes away, for the Vatican and Piazza di Spagna; for the Colosseum change at Termini to Line B
  • Restaurants just steps away (Perilli al Flaminio, Bistrot il Cortile del Politecnico 50 m)
  • Self check-in and check-out (check-in from 3 pm, check-out by 11 am), elevator to upper floors
  • Free cancellation up to 5 days before arrival (short stays, flexible rate)
  • Public parking nearby

Live Flaminio

We're set between the greenery of Villa Borghese and the elegance of Parioli, just steps from Piazza del Popolo and Rome's Renaissance heart. A residential, well-kept neighborhood, where the contemporary art of MAXXI, the music of the Auditorium Parco della Musica, and the energy of Stadio Olimpico meet along the Tiber, all the way to the charming Ponte Milvio. The Flaminio Metro stop puts the rest of the city just minutes away.
