BQA San Crisogono Apartments Rome-Trastevere

Looking for an apartment in Trastevere for your stay in Rome? Historic 80 sqm apartment in the heart of Trastevere: 19th-century terracotta floors, exposed beams and every modern comfort, steps away from authentic alleys and trattorias.

The property

  • Up to 4 guests
  • Living room with queen-size sofa bed, 55" 4K Smart TV and dining table for 4
  • Fully equipped kitchen: induction cooktop, refrigerator, coffee machine, microwave
  • Double bedroom (queen bed, 100% cotton linens) with 42" 4K Smart TV
  • Bathroom with stone shower, bidet and direct access to a private balcony
  • Workstation with desktop PC and high-speed WiFi
  • Air conditioning and heating throughout
  • Bathroom welcome kit
  • Crib available on request
  • Self check-in, 24/7 support by phone, SMS or email

The area

  • 450 m from Piazza di Santa Maria in Trastevere, the heart of the neighborhood
  • Campo de' Fiori and Largo di Torre Argentina about 1.1 km away
  • Piazza Navona, Trevi Fountain and the Colosseum within walking distance (1.4–2.2 km)
  • Traditional restaurants and osterie just steps away (Insalata Ricca and Convivium 50 m)
  • Circo Massimo metro station 1.6 km away
