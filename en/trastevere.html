BQA San Crisogono Apartments Rome-Trastevere

Looking for an apartment in Trastevere for your stay in Rome? Historic 80 sqm apartment on the second floor of a period building, in the heart of Trastevere but on a quiet street, away from the crowds: 19th-century terracotta floors, exposed beams and every modern comfort, with Roman trattorias practically downstairs.

The property

  • Best for 2-3 guests, up to 4
  • Two separate rooms: open-plan living room with a 150x200 double sofa bed, 4K Smart TV and dining table for 4
  • Bedroom with a 150x200 double bed, 100% cotton bed linen and 4K Smart TV
  • Fully equipped kitchen: refrigerator, induction cooktop, microwave, kettle, coffee machine, toaster
  • Bathroom with stone shower and bidet
  • Workstation with desk, PC and fast wi-fi
  • Air conditioning and heating throughout
  • Washing machine and bath welcome kit
  • Crib available on request
  • 24/7 self check-in with clear instructions; we are always reachable remotely, by phone, SMS or email
  • Good to know: historic building, second floor, no elevator (two flights of stairs)
  • Good to know: the balcony is small, a service balcony reached from the bathroom; room for two people standing, not meant for dining

The area

  • In the heart of Trastevere but on a quiet street, away from the crowds
  • 450 m from Piazza di Santa Maria in Trastevere, the heart of the neighborhood
  • On foot in 15-25 minutes: Campo de' Fiori, Piazza Navona, Pantheon, Imperial Forums
  • Trevi Fountain and Colosseum about 2 km away (30-35 minutes on foot)
  • Roman trattorias practically downstairs
  • Circo Massimo metro station 1.6 km away
  • In the morning you may hear the bells of the Basilica of Santa Cecilia
